How priority works

Every hackathon gets a score out of 100: how soon registration closes (up to 55), the prize money (up to 35) and how many people have registered (up to 10).

High
Score 60 or more
Medium
Score 35 to 59
Low
Score under 35

Closing within 3 days earns the most points and a closed one earns none. Prizes are converted to rupees so every currency compares fairly. A deadline that is only a guess counts for less.

About

Every hackathon, before the deadline.

Hackathon Finder is one index that scrapes Devpost, Unstop, college notices and community channels — then ranks what it finds by what actually matters to you: your region, your level, prize size, and how soon each one closes.

It started from a simple problem — good hackathons get discovered too late, buried across a dozen scattered sources. Hackathon Finder pulls them into one place and pushes an alert before the deadline, so you never miss one you'd have wanted to enter.
